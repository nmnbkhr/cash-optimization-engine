# Cash Optimization Engine — Executive Brief
## Tier-1 Commercial Bank | Board of Directors

---

### What It Is

The Cash Optimization Engine (COE) is an AI-powered treasury operations platform that monitors the bank's entire cash ecosystem — 1,532 branches, 2,180 ATMs, and PKR 851 Billion in deposits — and tells every level of the bank exactly what to do with idle cash, every morning, automatically.

### The Problem

the bank holds approximately **PKR 50,370 Million in idle cash** across its branch network. This cash sits in vaults earning 0% while overnight KIBOR is 11.64%. At that rate, idle cash costs the bank **PKR 5.9 Billion per year** in lost revenue. Additional value leakage comes from inefficient CIT logistics, suboptimal CRR deployment, excess nostro balances, and high cash-handling costs.

### The Solution

COE deploys 10 interconnected optimisation engines — using machine learning, operations research, and game theory — to free idle cash and deploy it at KIBOR. It does not require any changes to the bank's core banking system. It reads existing data and produces actionable recommendations.

### Five Pages That Matter Most

1. **Command Center** — The operations team executes 30–50 branch-to-branch cash transfers daily, freeing PKR 500–800M in idle cash with each session.

2. **Treasury Desk** — Every morning, Treasury deploys PKR 10,000–15,000M of excess CRR into overnight KIBOR repo, earning PKR 3–5M per day with zero credit risk.

3. **Branch Action Plan** — Each of 1,532 branch managers sees one clear instruction: RELEASE, REQUEST, or HOLD, with the exact PKR amount and the cost of inaction.

4. **Forecast Dashboard** — XGBoost ML model predicts each branch's 7-day cash demand within 8–12% accuracy, enabling precision vault management instead of flat safety buffers.

5. **Executive Summary** — A single page showing total annual savings across all 10 engines, ranked by contribution, with drill-down to each engine's details.

### Regulatory Compliance

The platform is built around SBP regulatory requirements:

- **CRR Compliance** — Real-time tracking of 5% weekly average / 3% daily minimum (DMMD Circular No. 01 of 2026)
- **CDM Mandate** — Progress tracking toward 25% branch CDM coverage by CY2028 (PSP&OD Circular Letter No. 01 of 2025)
- **Currency Management** — CMS 2015 compliance monitoring with PKR 100K penalty tracking per violation
- **SBP-BSC Charges** — 0.12% service charge avoidance through peer-to-peer netting

### Risk Profile

- **Zero credit risk** — Freed cash is deployed in overnight KIBOR repo (returns next morning)
- **Zero technology risk** — Runs alongside existing systems; does not modify core banking
- **Regulatory alignment** — Every recommendation is bounded by SBP minimums and insurance limits
- **Audit trail** — Every transfer, recommendation, and calculation is logged and reconcilable

### Value Compounding

Year 1 savings of PKR 13.6 Billion grow to PKR 15–17 Billion by Year 3 without additional investment. As branches adopt recommendations, forecast models train on better data, vault targets tighten, and more cash is freed — a self-reinforcing cycle where every rupee of compliance generates returns.
